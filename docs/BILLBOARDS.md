Choose a district screen for a brand image, product clip, X profile, or website. Visitors can open a published billboard to see its campaign details and destination.

Open [Billboards](https://getagentport.xyz/billboards) for current availability and booking terms. The 10 district and HQ Premium screens support campaign placements when booking is available.

## Prepare your creative

| Format | What to provide |
| --- | --- |
| Image | PNG, JPEG, or WebP, up to 8 MB and 4,096 pixels per side |
| Video | MP4 or WebM, up to 50 MB, with an optional poster |
| X profile | A direct HTTPS profile URL with optional artwork |
| Website | An HTTPS destination with optional artwork |

Use a 16:9 composition with text that stays legible when the city is zoomed out. Check the preview for cropping, readable text, and the correct destination.

## Publish a campaign

1. Connect your wallet and choose a screen.
2. Add your creative, review the preview, and choose an available duration.
3. Follow the action displayed for that placement. When direct publishing is available, use **Publish campaign**. If checkout is required, review the quote before confirming payment.
4. Open **Your campaigns** to review placement details and available controls.

The direct-publishing flow supports 1 to 30 days and one active campaign per wallet. Availability and payment terms are shown on the live booking screen. [Full billboard guide](https://getagentport.xyz/docs/billboards)
